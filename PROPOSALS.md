# SMART Splatting: research findings + 10 proposed features

_2026-07-10. Eight parallel Opus 5 research agents, ~872k tokens, 2024-2026 sources (dated inline). Working name "Nimbus3D" is not final; nothing here bakes it in._

## The one headline that reframes everything

**Your camera positions are the number one cause of every quality problem you named.** Your poses are raw ARKit VIO with no bundle adjustment, over a 74 m / 9.6 min walk. Measured against laser ground truth, raw ARKit poses on iPhone room scans are off by about 0.55 degrees and 19 mm (arXiv 2608.21008, Aug 2026). At your focal length (fx = 1346 px) that is roughly 13 pixels of misalignment at 2 m. Gaussian splatting needs sub-pixel agreement between frames. You are about 10x over budget. The optimiser's only way to satisfy contradictory frames is to paint a blur, so soft edges, ghosting at revisited viewpoints, and a doubled far field are mostly pose error, not sensor error.

Critical consequence: **every trust score, cluster check, or edge technique run on today's poses will measure your own drift and mislabel it as sensor noise.** Fix poses first. Then everything else becomes measurable.

Published payoff (same paper, 3DGS on iPhone room captures): ARKit poses 16.55 dB, refined poses 17.48 dB, ground truth 18.29 dB. So a good refinement recovers about half to two thirds of the gap. PSNR understates it, because the error is concentrated (ghosting at revisits) rather than spread.

**Trap to avoid:** the obvious plan, "run COLMAP triangulate-then-bundle-adjust seeded by ARKit," made poses WORSE in 15 of 15 ScanNet++ rooms (same paper). Structure gets triangulated from the drifted prior before anything is optimised, baking the error in. The fix that works is triangulation-free (structureless) bundle adjustment with a robust annealed loss, plus loop closure.

## Honest verdicts on your seven ideas

1. **Dual path (COLMAP as if no LiDAR + LiDAR, trust arbiter):** philosophy correct and already validated (SenFuNet ECCV 2022 built exactly a learned per-voxel ToF-vs-stereo arbiter; Double-Helix Active Geometry, arXiv 2607.02561, Jul 2026, adds per-region abstention). Mechanism wrong in two ways: (a) a from-scratch COLMAP reconstruction is hours of compute for a second opinion you can get in minutes, lives in an arbitrary scale/frame you then have to align to LiDAR, and likely fragments on textureless walls plus your 50-degree dead zone; (b) "two reconstructions voting per region" creates a trust-boundary seam. Stronger form: ONE solver, THREE witnesses (VIO pose prior, image matches, LiDAR depth) fused as weighted residuals with a robust loss. That IS your trust scoring, expressed the way 40 years of estimation theory does it.
2. **30x30 cm cluster accuracy scoring:** right data structure, wrong resolution, weakest link is the neighbour check. 30 cm straddles table edges (edges are a 1-5 cm problem), so a cell containing an edge scores "medium," the worst answer. Neighbour agreement measures stability, not truth: LiDAR errors (multipath, dark/shiny, grazing angle, Apple's upsampling) are spatially correlated, so wrong neighbours agree with each other. Stronger form: two scales. Coarse cells (25-50 cm) estimate BIAS (a whole region shifted = drift or multipath). Per-sample estimates NOISE/outliers (flying pixels, glass) and is never averaged. Independent evidence must come from a different modality (photometry), time (revisits), or channel (free space).
3. **Smart edges:** correct diagnosis, incomplete mechanism, and your own capture pipeline is causing part of it. Five causes of blur, ranked for YOUR data: pose error; the signed-gradient densification bug; the upsampled depth teaching a ramp where a step belongs; the built-in 0.3 px dilation; the Gaussian kernel itself. An edge loss alone attacks only the last. Where you are stronger than the literature: you have two independent edge sources (image = WHERE, LiDAR = WHAT), and nobody has built that asymmetric fusion.
4. **Smart background:** correct, and it is really two problems. Far diffuse geometry (long hallway) vs through-glass content (a separate layer at effectively infinity). The blown-out window is a CAPTURE problem: a clipped white pixel has no information to recover, no trainer fixes it. Also, "skyline" is the wrong frame: interiors see sky through a window aperture with tiny effective baseline, so the honest move is to snap it to infinity, not reconstruct it.
5. **No-training pre-pass:** your best idea; every agent said make it the centre of the architecture. But the target is wrong: your distances are already metric (LiDAR). The target is RELATIVE POSE CONSISTENCY between frames that see the same surface. Distances come for free once poses agree.
6. **Slice / train / merge via seams:** far better than you framed it, for a reason you did not state. Your scene FITS in 8 GB unsliced (LiDAR-initialised interiors converge at 1-2.5M splats; Structured-Li-GS reports 357k where vanilla needs 5x). On one GPU slicing gives zero speedup (every published win is multi-GPU). BUT slicing by TIME is a drift-absorbing structure: VIO is near-perfect over 20 s and poor over 10 min, so ~25 short submaps each internally correct collapse your drift into ~150 rigid parameters instead of 3402 (LoopSplat, 3DV 2025 Oral). "Train only the seams" can only fix 1 of 3 seam causes (misalignment, exposure drift, starvation at the cut). And "merge by distance" has a trap: overlapping translucent Gaussians ADD opacity, they do not average (VastGaussian hard-deletes outside the owner cell for this reason).
7. **Dynamic textures deprioritised:** every agent agreed. Also: extra appearance freedom is the easiest way for an optimiser to hide pose error. Keep appearance to a per-image exposure/gain, add it only after poses are locked.

## The 10 proposed features

### F1. Fix the poses first: time-sliced submaps + loop closure + LiDAR-anchored structureless BA
Cut the walk into ~25 windows of 15-30 s (20-30% overlap). Inside a window, VIO is essentially perfect, so freeze it. Find revisits with image retrieval (NetVLAD/MegaLoc via hloc; sequential-only matching never compares frame 60 to frame 480 looking at the same corner). Solve one rigid transform per submap with a pose graph (Open3D / GTSAM, seconds, CPU). Optionally a depth-anchored triangulation-free BA (arXiv 2608.21008: median 10 s on CPU, survives 16 degrees of prior error with annealed arctan loss) where each keypoint carries a LiDAR depth prior weighted by confidence, which fixes the near-zero-parallax degeneracy from walking at 0.11 m/s. Keep far, low-parallax matches: useless for depth, the BEST evidence for rotation (the term that costs fx x theta pixels regardless of distance). Then a constrained per-camera delta inside the trainer (frozen for the first ~5k iters, LR ~100x lower than positions, ideally a B-spline over time so it can only correct smooth drift). This subsumes your dual path (three witnesses, one solve) and your slicing idea (time slices). Difficulty: medium. Impact: largest single gain available; unblocks everything else.

### F2. "Empty air is evidence": LiDAR free-space carving
Every beam that hits a wall at 3 m proves the 3 m of air in front was empty. Build an occupancy grid (5 cm voxels, ~5M cells for a room, tens of MB) by carving all 567 frames' rays (28M rays, seconds on GPU). Every few hundred iterations, hard-delete any Gaussian whose centre sits in certified-empty space. Floaters are, by definition, exactly those Gaussians. Bonus: a beam that returns NOTHING at a window is a fact ("nothing solid within 5 m along this ray"), a lower bound that stops the outdoors being parked on the windowsill. Non-negotiable subtlety: mark beyond-range and no-return (glass) space as UNKNOWN, never EMPTY, or you delete the view through the window. Three agents independently called this the biggest overlooked win; no published 3DGS trainer does it with phone LiDAR beam paths (LI-GS, GS-SDF, GTLR-GS all use LiDAR as init + surface loss only). Difficulty: medium. Impact: probably the largest visual win; floaters are the number one complaint about phone splats.

### F3. Stop training on invented edges: native depth + edge band + the WHERE/WHAT fusion
Your 256x192 depth is guided-upsampled to 1920x1440; the crisp edges in it are drawn by Apple's filter, not measured (flying/mixed pixels). Export the NATIVE 256x192 depth + confidence (capture-app change), supervise only at the ~49k real samples per frame (27M constraints across 567 frames, zero invented). Compute depth edges on the native map, dilate by the upsample ratio (~8 px at full res), never supervise depth inside that band. Then the novel part only you can build: image gradients say WHERE an edge is; LiDAR says WHAT it is: GEOMETRIC (depth step), TEXTURE (painted stripe, flat depth), or UNKNOWN (glass/out of range/low confidence). Flatness pressure on texture edges, sharpening on geometric edges, nothing on unknown. Windows fall into "unknown" automatically. Also: DN-Splatter's exp(-grad I) weighting (WACV 2025) is the published baseline but it suppresses depth at ALL colour edges including real corners; the WHERE/WHAT split fixes that. Difficulty: easy (native + band) / medium (fusion). Impact: turns depth supervision from an edge-destroyer into an edge-helper.

### F4. Rasteriser truth: AbsGS, drop the dilation, SAD-GS anisotropic splits, bimodal edge supervision
(a) Vanilla 3DGS sums SIGNED per-pixel gradients to decide splitting; at an edge, left pushes left, right pushes right, they cancel, and the blob is judged fine exactly where it is worst (a tug-of-war measured by movement instead of tension). Take the absolute value first (AbsGS, arXiv 2404.10484; Pixel-GS adds pixel-area weighting). Two lines in a custom rasteriser. (b) Delete the 0.3 px dilation, add Mip-Splatting's 2D and 3D filters (CVPR 2024 best student paper), sizing the 3D filter by a high percentile of sampling rate rather than the max so your dead zone and thin ceiling are not over-smoothed. (c) SAD-GS (arXiv 2604.28016, Apr 2026, MPI): structure-tensor test, split ANISOTROPICALLY across the edge normal, ceil(sqrt(eta)) children, reports convergence in 3k iters instead of 30k (18-23x) with better SSIM/LPIPS. Spends your 2-3M budget on door frames, not flat wall. (d) Bimodal edge supervision (not found published for 3DGS): at a silhouette the honest depth is "both," so grade the rendered depth against whichever of the two local modes it picked, plus a transition-width penalty, instead of the mid-air average that creates boundary fog. Difficulty: easy (a, b) / medium (c, d). Impact: biggest visible sharpness gain per hour.

### F5. Three-regime background + parallax routing + the window fix at capture time
Near (0 to ~4.5 m): LiDAR authoritative. Mid (~4.5 to ~30 m): Prompt Depth Anything (CVPR 2025, arXiv 2412.14015, Apache-2.0) takes EXACTLY your inputs, a 192x256 ARKit LiDAR map plus RGB, and emits dense metric depth scale-locked to your LiDAR by construction; SOTA on ARKitScenes/ScanNet++; run once offline over 567 frames in minutes; trust it to ~8-12 m, past that use it for init and ordering only (AbsRel ~0.10 means +-3 m at 30 m). Far and apertures: a direction-only environment model (Street Gaussians cubemap or Splatfacto-W 3-layer direction MLP, ~50k params), composited behind everything, GLOBAL and FROZEN after warm-up so slices never invent different skies. Routing by a per-pixel authority map from: LiDAR validity, recalibrated confidence, a soft range ramp 4.5-5.5 m, semantic sky/window/glass mask (SegFormer on ADE20K), saturation mask, and a PARALLAX gate: if a patch was never viewed from more than ~0.5 degrees apart its distance is unknowable, send it to infinity (novel; catches far blank walls no sky detector flags). Glass detector for free: LiDAR-silent + image-bright + planar inside a planar wall. The window truth: clipped white is unrecoverable; fix at CAPTURE with exposure bracketing (every 5th frame ~3 stops darker via ARKit 6 manual exposure; verify it does not disturb VIO) plus a per-frame exposure parameter in the trainer. That is how "you can see the garden" happens. VRAM-POSITIVE: background out of the splat budget. Difficulty: medium. Impact: the whole window/far-field story; also a drift instrument (a 1-degree rotation error moves a 30 m object half a metre, so a doubled far field is your VIO shouting).

### F6. Trust scoring done right: two scales, a cheap photo second opinion, physics priors, structural trust
Coarse (25-50 cm) bias field + per-sample noise field (see verdict 2). Replace "run COLMAP" with a targeted plane-sweep test at each native LiDAR sample: assume the LiDAR depth, project the patch into 4-8 co-visible frames, ZNCC; sweep +-15 cm in 32 steps; record peak NCC (is there texture), peak offset (does photo agree, by how much), peak sharpness (confidence). ~10^11 MACs total, low single-digit minutes to ~30 min on a 4060, cached forever. Add a free physics prior: error grows with range and with grazing incidence angle (Kinect-era noise models). Recalibrate ARKit confidence: it flags only ~1.6% of points low and is materially miscalibrated (ECE ~0.22-0.28, arXiv 2607.02561); use it as a ranking remapped via your revisit residuals, never as the trust map. Soft inverse-variance weights, never hard switches (switches make trust seams). Per-frame learnable scale+shift on the SENSOR depth too (sensor warms up, calibration shifts; 2 params/frame, tightly constrained). Make trust STRUCTURAL: a splat born from a trusted sample starts thin, opaque, pinned; from a doubtful one, stretched along the ray, translucent, free (RGS-SLAM, GSCS 2026). Decaying depth-loss schedule: strong for the first ~3-7k iters, then taper, or LiDAR's centimetre bias becomes a permanent quality ceiling. Honest expectation: 0.1-2.3 dB on its own; its real job is making windows, edges, and far-field behave. Must run AFTER F1. Difficulty: medium.

### F7. Slicing, upgraded: doorway cuts, shared background, tied overlaps, the 7-number merge
Only needed for whole-house / multi-floor or to push past ~3M splats (your reference capture fits). When you do: partition by camera co-visibility clustering (OccluGaussian, ICCV 2025), which cuts at doorways automatically because photos in different rooms barely overlap, and the wall hides the seam; never a spatial grid. Share overlap cameras (VastGaussian's biggest ablation: dropping them costs LPIPS 0.121 -> 0.261). Balance blocks by visible-Gaussian count, not volume (a window or corridor sightline costs far more than its footprint). One block resident at a time (Momentum-GS, ICCV 2025, 5.8 GB peak). Tie overlap-band Gaussians with a consistency constraint DURING training (Computers & Graphics, Nov 2025), nearly free on one GPU. One global frozen background. Merge: delete every Gaussian outside its owner's unexpanded bounds and concatenate (one owner per region, no blending). Then the cheap global pass: freeze ALL geometry, optimise one Sim3 (7 numbers) per block plus per-image exposure/gain, a few thousand params, ~1 minute, cannot break anything. Only if a crack remains, densify the seam band. Difficulty: hard (full). Impact: a real whole-house path with seams prevented by construction.

### F8. Capture-side gold nobody logs (you own both ends)
(a) Camera-to-IMU time offset: one scalar. A 10 ms mislabel during a modest 20 deg/s turn is 0.2 degrees = 4.7 px on EVERY frame, a constant bias BA cannot cleanly absorb. Test: sweep -50..+50 ms in 5 ms steps, re-interpolate the pose stream, plot reprojection error, take the minimum. 20 minutes. Possibly the largest gain per line of code in this document. (b) Read ARKit anchor transforms at the END of the session: ARKit quietly corrects its past when it recognises a place, and logging at capture time throws the correction away; drop an anchor per keyframe, read back at the end, diff against the synchronous log (forum-grade evidence, 1-2 cm reported, cheap to test). (c) Per-frame QC weights into the dataset as a sidecar (turn rate, tracking state, exposure jump, blur, depth validity fraction) so the trainer downweights bad frames automatically; no open trainer consumes such a weight. (d) Log exposureDuration/exposureOffset and divide exposure out analytically (auto-exposure makes a wall 15% brighter look like a wall that CHANGED, and the optimiser explains it with a floating translucent sheet, i.e. floaters); then a tiny per-image affine (12 params) mops up; or lock exposure/WB at capture. (e) Log native depth + confidence, timestamps, angular velocity and readout time (rolling shutter later). Difficulty: easy. Impact: high, compounding, and the moat: a wrapper around someone else's trainer cannot do any of it.

### F9. In-room QC, "Fix this" navigation, honest preview (the UX)
Coverage painted on the room's own ARKit-classified surfaces in THREE channels: how many angles, how close the best look was, how sharp those frames were; each colour has a different fix (walk around / walk toward / slow down). A QC card in under 3 s from poses + LiDAR, no training: REAL drift in cm from revisit depth residuals ("your loop closes to 8 cm"; ARKit benchmarks at ~0.19 m loop drift, good news you can show), per-surface angular spread (the global azimuth ring is legible but can look perfect while one wall is only seen from one side), camera-to-surface distance, step/time outliers, look-direction (switches which thresholds apply), loop-closure count, height spread, glass area, ceiling coverage. Every defect gets a "Fix this" button: arrow on the floor, ghost outline in the air, walks you back, ticks itself off when the patch turns green. Audio/haptic as the PRIMARY channel (CHI 2024, Niantic Labs, n=50: more usable AND scans often MORE accurate; you cannot look at the screen while scanning a ceiling, and looking down is what makes people swing too fast; Niantic still has not shipped it). A live blur meter in pixels (gyro rate x exposure duration / 0.0426 deg per px; amber at 2 px, red at 4; lock shutter fast). Window mode: ARKit labels windows; say so, lock exposure, stand back, bracket, mark the region in the export. Coverage % as the "done" criterion, not a timer (guided capture: 224 images beat 346 free-capture images on every metric, arXiv 2604.19216). Preview: keep the virtual camera ON the walked path (within ~20 cm; off-path eye-level is out-of-distribution and produces floaters, arXiv 2510.20027) and WIDEN the lens to 100-110 degrees instead; hatch pixels no real photo ever looked at (honesty mask, precomputed from the same coverage grid). Photo-vs-splat A/B slider from ~5% held-out frames. Staged training progress with a fixed review camera; if sliced, a "walk the seams" path. Render the trust field as a fourth floorplan layer. Difficulty: easy-medium. Impact: no competitor ships any of it; this is the product.

### F10. Right-size the machine + the memory levers (train on PC, preview on phone)
Phone (A19 Pro): ~2.5 TFLOPS, ~76 GB/s, 67% sustained stability (throttles hard), jetsam ~5-7 GB usable. PC (4060): ~15-20 TFLOPS, 272 GB/s. Splatting is bandwidth/atomics-bound, so plan on ~3.6-5x PC advantage. PocketGS (iPhone 15, Metal, Jan 2026): 500 iters, 168k splats, 255 s, 2.2 GB. Your full 567-frame train on-phone: hours to overnight, throttled, likely killed. Not viable in 2026. Apple's "3x GPU" is matmul neural accelerators; splatting is sorting/scattering; budget ~40%. Split: phone = capture, pre-pass (5-20 s), live coverage, 60-120 s preview splat (500-2000 iters, 150-400k splats, thermal-gated via ProcessInfo.thermalState), final viewing; PC = every real train (naive 45-90 min; with levers 10-25 min; with DashGaussian schedule + masked Adam plausibly 6-15 min). Levers: SH degree 3 -> 1 or 2 (48 of 59 numbers per splat are view-dependent colour; a matte room barely needs them; = 2.5-4x more splats free) and fp16 for them; visibility-masked/sparse Adam (a 71-degree frame sees 5-15% of the room; stop re-stamping the rest; gsplat sparse_grad); budget-first densification with a hard cap (Taming-3DGS / MCMC; declare 2M, spend by value, no OOM lottery); coarse-to-fine frequency training (Opti3DGS: 62% fewer Gaussians, 40% less VRAM); stream images from pinned host RAM (567 frames at 1280 px = 2.1 GB, must never sit in VRAM); recompute tile lists in backward instead of storing. Host-memory offloading (GS-Scale: 4M -> 18M on an 8 GB laptop GPU at 1.22x speed) if you ever need it. 8 GB is NOT your binding constraint. Difficulty: easy. Impact: makes 8 GB comfortable, removes the memory motive for slicing.

## How to measure honestly (fits how you test)
PSNR/SSIM are dominated by flat regions and will barely move for edge work. Use: masked PSNR/LPIPS inside dilated edge bands; gradient-magnitude correlation; held-out REVISIT frames (a random held-out video frame has a neighbour 30 ms away and is nearly free to predict, which is the tolerated cheat in the field); rendered-depth-vs-LiDAR error on high-confidence pixels as a geometry referee independent of the photometric loss (if held-out PSNR rises while depth error rises, the pose refinement is cheating); and negative controls (apply the edge stack to an edge-free region, confirm nothing changes; test pose refinement in isolation before any edge work).
